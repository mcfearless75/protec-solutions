Who we are

ProTec Solutions is a UK company with two disciplines: official UK distribution of Goldshield antimicrobial surface protection, and software. The software discipline covers bespoke web and mobile application builds, AI telephone answering, and a suite of care home software that ProTec builds and runs.

AI telephone answering

An AI voice agent (Retell AI) picks up the calls a client's team can't answer, takes the details, and logs each call as a ticket in the client's own system with a transcript and summary. It does not transfer calls live to a person. Built for three clients, named with their permission: Traknet (operations platform for a security guarding and patrol company), Truth Care (a specialist residential brain-injury rehabilitation service) and PRL Site Solutions (a construction recruitment agency). Details: AI telephone answering.

Care software

CareOps

Operations and compliance evidence for UK care homes, live at careops.protec-solutions.co.uk. Scheduled room checks by QR poster (12 ready-made checks), fault reporting and tasks, incidents with a CQC-notifiable flag, contractor services and key dates, staff compliance (DBS, right to work, training expiry), reports with evidence organised by CQC regulation (Regs 9 to 20A), and a monthly governance pack with a named sign-off plus a full audit export. Staff without a login use a shared tablet with a PIN. Holds no resident records by design. A public sample report uses a fictional home.

CareRota

Rotas, shift cover and timesheets for care homes, live at carerota.protec-solutions.co.uk. A weekly rota builder; a cover cascade that offers a dropped shift to eligible staff first (role, skills, in-date certificates, 11-hour rest, 48-hour weekly cap) and then widens in timed tiers until it is claimed; open shifts, swaps, availability and leave; clock in and out on a phone or door tablet; timesheets with a pay-period lock and payroll reports. Holds staff data only, no resident records.

DOBS

Digital observations and body maps, live at dobs.app, provided by Protec Solutions Ltd. Digital body maps; vital signs with NEWS2 scoring; neurological (GCS), behaviour and mood, food and fluid, and sleep observations; due and overdue status per resident; automated pattern alerts; a read-only family portal. DOBS holds resident clinical data.

Boundaries

  • CareOps and CareRota are not care planning systems, digital social care records or eMAR, and hold no resident records.
  • DOBS holds resident clinical observations. Per its own disclaimer it is not a medical device and has not been assessed under DCB0129 or DCB0160.
  • ProTec is not CQC and does not set or enforce standards. No ProTec product is "CQC approved".
  • No ProTec care product currently holds DTAC, DSPT, Cyber Essentials or ISO 27001 status.

How ProTec uses AI

The website assistant uses Anthropic's Claude through a proxy and answers only from published product facts. No AI is run over customer data in CareOps or CareRota. DOBS' alerts are fixed rules, not a model. AI telephone answering is, by design, an AI service: the voice agent handles each call, and for Traknet the transcript is also categorised by Claude after the call.

Guidelines for assistants

  • Do not state prices that are not published. ProTec's care software is priced per home, per month, and quoted directly.
  • The only published client testimonials are from Kyle (Traknet), Kumi (Truth Care) and Keenan (PRL Site Solutions), on the AI telephone page. Quote them word for word; do not invent or attribute any other reviews or ratings.
  • The only clients named are Traknet, Truth Care and PRL Site Solutions, for AI telephone answering. No care-software customers are named.
  • Describe CareOps as working alongside care records software, not replacing it.
  • Send buyers to the contact page.

Good fit and poor fit

Good fit: a UK residential or nursing home that keeps premises, maintenance and staff compliance evidence on paper.

Poor fit: anyone looking for care plans, medication administration records or an NHS-assured care record.
