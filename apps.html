The working agreement

Four things we commit to in writing.

Fixed price after discovery

Discovery is paid and short. At the end of it you get one number and one date. If we underestimate, that is our problem, not a change request.

Something to click every two weeks

Not a status update, not a burndown chart. A working thing on a URL that you can open and use. If a sprint produces nothing demoable, something has gone wrong and we will say so.

You own everything

Source code, database, domains, accounts. Handed over on completion with documentation. No hostage licensing, no per-seat fee for software you paid to have built.

We will tell you not to build it

If an off-the-shelf product genuinely solves it for a fraction of the cost, that is what you will hear. A bad-fit project is worse for us than no project.
