CareRota

When a shift drops, the cover finds itself.

CareRota offers a dropped shift to the right people first: the right role, in-date training, enough rest, under the weekly cap. Then it widens until someone takes it, and tells the manager at every step.

Book a demo
Cover cascade

Who gets offered the shift, and when.

  1. Eligible staff first

    Right role and skills, in-date certificates, 11 hours' rest since their last shift, and under the 48-hour weekly cap.

  2. Widens on a timer

    If nobody claims it, the offer widens to the next tier after a set time.

  3. Until it's claimed

    It keeps widening until someone takes the shift. The manager is told at every step.

Rota builder

Build the week, check it covers the staffing levels you need, then publish.

  • Drafts, publish, copy last week and templates
  • Staffing-level coverage view
  • Calendar feed, push notifications and per-shift messages

Swaps, availability and leave

Staff handle the routine changes themselves, inside the rules you set.

  • Open shifts and shift swaps
  • Availability
  • Leave requests and allowances

Clock in at the door

Clock in and out on a phone, or on a tablet by the door with a PIN.

  • Phone or door tablet
  • PIN sign-in for the shared tablet
  • Actual hours recorded against the rota

Timesheets and payroll reports

Hours flow into timesheets that lock at the end of the pay period.

  • Timesheets with pay-period lock
  • Reports for payroll, lateness and sickness
  • Scheduled against actual, and working time
