How it runs

Four steps. No six-month discovery phase.

01

Survey or discovery

For protection, we walk the site and measure. For software, we sit with the people who do the job and map what actually happens, not what the process document claims.

02

Fixed proposal

One number, one timeline, one scope. If something is genuinely uncertain we say so and price the unknown separately rather than padding the whole thing.

03

Delivery

Treatment scheduled outside operating hours. Software in two-week sprints with something you can click at the end of every one.

04

Evidence & handover

Certification pack for the audit file, or full source code and documentation for the software. Either way you own what you paid for.
