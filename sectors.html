Why both

The protection is the service. The software is the proof.

Nobody gets audited on how clean a surface felt. They get audited on whether they can produce a dated record showing what was treated, with what, by whom, and when it is due again.

Treatment logged at source

The operative scans the zone, the app records product, batch, area and timestamp. No paperwork to transcribe later, no gaps discovered at audit.

Re-treatment scheduled automatically

Every zone carries its own expiry based on the product applied. The schedule builds itself and warns you before anything lapses.

Certificates on demand

An inspector asks for evidence on a Tuesday morning. You produce it from your phone in under a minute, covering every zone and every visit.
